# Goal progress — 2026-07-31

- Corrected bot discard evaluation for game contracts: once the auction fixes
  the trump strain, the remaining hand is now scored against that declared
  strain instead of whichever alternate suit happens to look strongest. A
  focused exchange-state corpus pins the contract-specific discard and the
  full bot simulation remains legal and terminating.
- Corrected two-whister responsibility for six-trick contracts: each
  defender now pays every trick missing from their half of the four-trick
  quota, rather than being capped at one mountain unit. A zero-trick
  two-whister defense is pinned at two missing units per defender.
- Gave the terminal match screen a true size-class split: iPhone keeps the
  compact result-to-actions flow, while regular iPad places standings beside
  a dedicated action column; Accessibility text sizes intentionally stack
  again to protect label readability. A pure layout policy regression pins all
  three branches.
- Split the scored-deal phase by device as well: regular iPad now keeps the
  trick/result summary beside its score-delta and next-deal controls, while
  iPhone and Accessibility text retain the readable vertical flow. The pure
  layout policy regression pins all three branches.
- Closed a recovered-state talon invariant: after exchange, the historical
  talon must be traceable to the declarer’s hand, discard, or played cards in
  every contract phase. A forged multiplayer snapshot with unrelated talon
  cards now fails validation before it can reach projection or scoring.
- Froze the worker roster at the lobby boundary: play cannot start with a
  pending seat, late accounts cannot claim seats after `playing`, and host bot
  filling is rejected outside the lobby. Focused worker regressions pin all
  three paths so backend lifecycle and frontend readiness cannot diverge.
- Serialized host mutations across engine apply, durable state reporting, and
  projection fan-out. A second action can no longer overtake a network-stalled
  first action or publish an older sequence after a newer one; detached host
  epochs cancel queued work. Focused queue regressions pin ordering and cancel
  behavior.
- Made live-table abandonment use the same terminal boundary: it cancels bot
  and queued host work, invalidates non-cooperative in-flight reports, commits
  `abandoned`, then disconnects the old authority. A suspended-move regression
  proves no gameplay projection appears after the terminal write begins.
- Closed a multiplayer action-ordering hole: every host action now must be
  based on the exact projection sequence the client saw, so stale bids, whists,
  and card plays fail without mutating the authoritative engine. Duplicate
  `startDeal` retries remain idempotent, negative resync cursors are rejected,
  and a host's own resync restores projection, activity, bot notes, and table
  state together. Focused event-sourcing, policy, and single-whist regressions
  pin the invariant.
- Made bot contract risk visible in play: a weak high-level declaration now
  uses the supported without-three concession when its expected trick count is
  clearly below the target, while bold/expert bots keep a narrower risk margin
  and strong hands still declare normally. Fixed weak/strong declaration
  scenarios pin the policy and its legal action.
- Closed the clean-break realtime boundary: every hello, client action, seat
  assignment, projection, host error, resync request, and ping now requires
  the current game-wire schema. Missing or pre-v2 frames fail closed at both
  JSON decoding and coordinator policy, so an old client cannot attach to a
  fresh room even when its seat identity happens to look valid.
- Corrected gentleman-whist remise scoring for two whisting defenders: the
  defending trick remuneration is now split evenly between both defenders,
  while each convention's separate declarer-remise consolation remains intact.
  Rostov and Leningrad regressions pin the published convention.
- Reset the compact iPhone auction rail when the projected table state

  opening bid. Headless auction, prikup, and redesign flows pass on both
  iPhone and iPad.
- Corrected the remaining Rostov whist-responsibility mismatch: a failed
