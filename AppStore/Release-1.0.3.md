# Preferans 1.0.3 (29)

Prepared 31 October 2026 for TestFlight, not a public App Store release.

## Suggested What's New

Online play now connects to our new server. Create public tables, find other
players and join a table with open seats. Confirm Ready together before starting.
We also improved room creation feedback, invitations and reconnect handling.

All players need the new version to play together. Previous online rooms and
guest history remain on the previous server and are not automatically transferred.
