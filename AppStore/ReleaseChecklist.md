# Release checklist

- Confirm bundle identifier, signing team, version and build number.
- Test local play and online rooms on physical devices with separate accounts.
- Verify privacy answers against the shipped app using `PrivacyChecklist.md`.
- Review `Metadata.json`, `ReviewNotes.md`, support and legal URLs.
- Capture fresh screenshots of the release build; existing screenshot assets are historical.
- Archive and validate the signed build before upload.
- Record uploaded build provenance in `BuildHistory.md`.
