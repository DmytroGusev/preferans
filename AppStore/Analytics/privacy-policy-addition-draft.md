## Optional usage analytics

Guru of Cards offers optional usage analytics through Google Analytics for
Firebase to understand how Preferans is used and improve reliability and the
playing experience. Analytics stays off unless you choose Allow analytics.
Declining does not restrict offline or online play. You can change your choice
under Settings > Usage analytics.

If you agree, Google processes an SDK-generated app-instance identifier,
app/device/operating-system information, event times, sessions and engagement,
approximate geographic information derived from your network address, and
events about onboarding, screens, local/online play, room operations, bot seats,
connection states and completed deals/matches. Gameplay fields are limited to
mode, number of players/bots, convention, bot difficulty, result type and deal
count. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not anonymous.

We do not send your name, email, game-account identifier, room code, invitation
link, cards, individual scores or raw error messages to Firebase Analytics.
We do not assign your game-account ID as an analytics user ID. Advertising-ID
and IDFV collection are disabled. We do not use this integration to track you
across other companies' apps or websites for advertising, and advertising
storage, advertising user data and personalization consent remain denied.
This integration does not include Crashlytics or Performance Monitoring.

Turning analytics off stops collection and resets this device's analytics
app-instance identifier. Successful online-account deletion also turns it off.
This does not retroactively erase previously processed Google reports or
aggregates. Account/game-data deletion remains available separately in Settings.
For privacy questions or requests, contact support@guruofcards.com. Google may
process analytics data internationally under its applicable terms and safeguards;
see [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

The property's settings, verified on 3 November 2026, retain event-level data
for two months and user-level data for fourteen months, with the latter period
reset after new user activity. Standard aggregated reports are not governed by
those same retention limits. Before publication, recheck these settings and
review the Google account-level data-sharing settings; do not promise immediate
remote deletion or a retention limit for all aggregates.
