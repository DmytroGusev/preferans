Preferans 1.0.3 (31). This is the non-tracking privacy correction build.

PRIVACY CORRECTION: The unused App Tracking Transparency request, IDFA helper, first-launch request and tracking Settings controls have been removed. NSUserTrackingUsageDescription and the AppTrackingTransparency/AdSupport framework dependencies are absent from the verified signed IPA. No advertising, analytics, attribution or third-party crash-reporting SDK is bundled. No ATT prompt is expected because this build does not track users across other companies' apps or websites. Online account and gameplay information is used for multiplayer functionality, not advertising.

LOCAL PLAY: Choose Play with bots in the lobby, configure the table and start. Follow bidding, talon exchange, manual two-card discard, contract declaration, defense and trick-play prompts. Scoring is automatic. Three players or four with a rotating dealer are supported.

ONLINE PLAY: Choose Play online, enter a display name and tap Continue as Guest, or use Sign in with Apple. No demo credentials, purchase or subscription is required. Create a public or invite-only table. On a second device using build 30 or 31 on the owner-hosted service, use Find a table or the room code/invite. Public-table players confirm Ready; the host starts. Use Your games to resume with the same account. Room codes from older releases' separate service are not interchangeable.

MULTIPLAYER: Includes reconnect recovery, next-deal progress with an offline manager, private account-ID masking, finished-game history scores and ended-table retention from build 30. The server has not been changed for build 31.

BOT SCOPE: Local bot practice is supported. Per-seat online waiting-room bot controls remain a separate unshipped feature. There is no Club subscription purchase or monthly online quota in this build.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Gear button > Settings > Account > Delete online account, then confirm. This permanently deletes the server account/game library, invalidates sessions and removes the player's identity from rooms. Shared results can retain anonymized seats and scores. Sign-out or uninstalling is not account deletion.
