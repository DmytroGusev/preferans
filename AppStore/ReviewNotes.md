# App Review Notes Draft

## Demo Summary

Preferans is a card game for 3 to 4 players. The submitted build supports local game setup and a simplified hand flow based on Preferans.

## Reviewer Instructions

1. Launch the app.
2. Choose Online mode.
3. Enter any display name in the You section. Sign in with Apple is optional.
4. Tap Create invite link to create an online room, or paste an invite code/link and tap Join.
5. To verify account deletion, open Settings from the gear button, then use Account → Delete account data and confirm.
6. For local gameplay, choose Local mode, tap Start Hand, bid or pass, take the talon when prompted, and play cards from the hand to complete tricks.

## Notes

- The app does not track users across apps or websites and does not include advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting, or data broker SDKs.
- App Privacy in App Store Connect should be configured as No Tracking for this build.
- Online room identity is used only for multiplayer room participation.
- Account deletion is available in-app from Settings → Account → Delete account data.
